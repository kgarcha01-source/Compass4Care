# Compass4Care — Family Version

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** A calmer, safer path through care transitions for Ontario families, with choice remaining in their hands.
- **Identity:** 16:9 landscape; C4R/C4C reference palette of evergreen, warm paper, and clay; Newsreader-like editorial serif paired with DM Sans.
- **Hero:** A single continuous navigation line, first tangled among family-care fragments, then becoming a route, a consent boundary, and finally a safety net.
- **Motion:** Tangled-line unwinding, masked typographic reveals, and compound aperture / split-panel match cuts.
- **Arc:** Uneasy complexity → clear tool → three practical reassurances → quiet, confident lockup.
- **Payoff:** The final route folds into a generous, calm end card, with the visual QR placeholder explicitly marked as non-scannable.
- **Runtime:** 32 seconds, 16:9.
- **Brand inputs:** Existing C4R/C4C transition-support site is the visual reference: deep green, warm cream, muted clay, DM Sans and Newsreader. No verified official Compass4Care logo/site was found; use the name as typeset text, not a fabricated logo. No destination URL was supplied for a QR code; show only a clearly labelled visual placeholder.

## Shot-by-shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–4.00s / 4.00s — “WHEN CARE CHANGES.”
**Purpose:** Recognize the emotional friction of a family transition without dramatizing or promising a clinical outcome.

**Composition and artistic idea:** A deep-green close crop of a warm-paper route map. Three thin clay and cream routes enter from different edges, knot briefly around small hand-drawn waypoint circles, then resolve into one unbroken line. The opening focal point is the knot at 54% x / 50% y; the line grows to occupy the frame width, with the phrase anchored in a safe lower-left field. Low-contrast paper grain and soft side light keep the abstraction tactile.

**Exact content and holds:** “WHEN CARE CHANGES.” appears at 0.42s and remains to 3.72s. “A clearer way forward for Ontario families.” appears at 1.55s and remains to 3.72s.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.48s: three route fragments spring-snap into the knot, one every 0.22s; the first little route dot is visible immediately. 0.45–1.20s: the knot unwinds into a continuous line, with a short stiff overshoot at 0.92s. 0.55–1.00s: headline is masked upward. 1.25–2.10s: the line traces past the headline and settles as an underline without crossing text. 2.10–3.25s: faint nodes breathe in sequence from left to right; the camera pulls back slightly to reveal the map-like field. 3.25–4.00s: line curls into an oval aperture; headline shears gently upward as the oval crosses the frame edge.

**Transition contract:** The outgoing oval is the incoming card's circular seal; preserve its size and position through a brief overlapping scale and paper-color reveal.

**Assets and technique:** CSS/SVG route paths, circles, paper texture and grain; no logo or external image required. Newsreader + DM Sans via existing scaffold fonts.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental cinematic minimal piano with soft strings, establishing warmth rather than urgency. A gentle low percussion accent on the unwind; planned onset alignment unverified until audio generation.
